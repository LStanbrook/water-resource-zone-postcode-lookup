Frequently asked questions

What is a Water Resource Zone?

The area a water company plans and balances its supply for as one system — everyone in a zone shares the same risk of supply shortfall.

Where does the data come from?

Zone boundaries from MOSL (128 zones) plus the Environment Agency's Isles of Scilly zone; postcodes from the ONS Postcode Directory (August 2026). Each postcode is placed in the zone containing its grid reference, and cross-checked against MOSL's own sector lookup — 97.6% agree exactly.

What do High, Medium and Low confidence mean?

  • High — inside the zone, and confirmed by MOSL's sector table.
  • Medium — inside the zone, but MOSL's table disagrees or doesn't cover it (or, for coordinates, within 50 m of a zone edge).
  • Low — just outside every zone boundary, so the nearest zone was used.
  • No match — invalid, not found, or outside England & Wales.

About the batch converter

Paste a list or upload a CSV of postcodes or latitude/longitude coordinates — up to 200,000 rows. Any other columns in your file are kept, with the results added on the end. Coordinates are matched against slightly simplified boundaries (~30 m), so check points near a zone edge on the map. Everything runs in your browser; your file is never uploaded.

Why can't my postcode be found?

It may be mistyped, terminated, or newer than August 2026. Scotland and Northern Ireland aren't covered.
